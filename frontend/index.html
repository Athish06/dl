Evolutionary Progression

Evolution of dl (V1 vs V2 vs V3)

dl evolved through three distinct deep learning generations, systematically advancing from single-task sentiment classification to multi-task learning, and finally to gated polarity inversion for resolving subtle sarcasm and mock praise.

Generation 1: Baseline Single-Task

Trained with a basic CNN-BiLSTM-Attention backbone (4.75M parameters). Features a whitespace tokenizer. While effective on direct sentiment, it lacks sarcasm awareness and systematically misclassifies sarcastic mock praise as positive sentiment.

Generation 2: Multi-Task Incongruity

Trained with GloVe 300d embeddings (10.61M parameters). Introduced a Rust BPE tokenizer (30,000 subwords), 4-scale parallel CNNs (k=2,3,5,7), and sequence incongruity detection. Added a dedicated sarcasm head but still suffered from mock praise confusion when surface positive tokens were dominant.

Generation 3: Gated Polarity Inversion

Trained on the hybrid corpus with GloVe 300d embeddings (10.66M parameters). Introduces a multiplicative Gated Polarity Inversion head: when sarcasm is detected ($p_{sarc} \approx 1$), the feature multiplier flips to $-1$, dynamically inverting surface positive features to negative sentiment.

Comparative Multi-Model Architecture

All three models are executed simultaneously in memory using PyTorch. Side-by-side evaluation provides empirical verification of architectural progression across generations.
